The engine

Four feedings, each built as protein (35–60g) + a real starch + a fat-carrying sauce or finish. The sauce is where the surplus hides — browned butter, tahini, aioli, chilli oil, a proper pan reduction. Calories you want to eat beat calories you have to force down.

Lunch & around training

Lunch is almost always last night's dinner plus extra rice — that's the point of cooking six portions of ragù. Around training, keep it fast:

  • Two slices sourdough + 40g peanut butter + honey, pre-lift
  • Whey in water straight after, real food within 90 minutes
  • Greek yogurt, granola, jam if the day is running short

The one habit

Cook Sunday like a restaurant does prep: a pot of rice, a batch of ragù or braise, a tray of roast veg, a jar of one good sauce, proteins portioned and seasoned raw. Weeknights become fifteen minutes of actual cooking — the only version of this you'll still be doing in week eleven.

How to progress

Pick muscles, not days

Any combination works — chest + back, back + arms, whatever you feel like. What drives growth is each muscle getting roughly twice a week, with a day or two between hits. The Muscles card tracks both: aim to get every bar past its tick, and avoid anything marked Recovering unless you're only doing light isolation work.

Double progression

Climb the rep range, then add weight. Bench example: 175 × 8, 8, 9 → 9, 9, 10 → when all three sets hit 10, go to 180 and start back at the bottom. 2.5–5 lb jumps upper, 5–10 lb lower. The ↑ means you beat last time.

Reps in reserve

Compounds: stop 1–2 reps short, last set can go to failure. Isolations: 0–1 in reserve is fine — they cost little recovery.

Rest

At least one full rest day every 6 sessions — the app flags when you're due. Take one early if lifts go backwards two sessions in a row; that's fatigue, not weakness.

Running

Your 2 miles a week costs about 200 calories — nothing. Put it on a rest day or after an upper session, never the day before legs.

Deload

Every 6–8 weeks: one week at ~60% of your working weights, same reps, calories unchanged.
